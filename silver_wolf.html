Silver Wolf

"Can you let me have some fun this time?"
★★★★★

Character Summary

Silver Wolf is a single target-oriented debuffer unit that provides RES down, DEF shred, and other minor debuffs (SPD and ATK down) to a specific enemy target. While her DEF shred is an AoE debuff, the rest of her debuffs are primarily single-target focused.

Most notably, Silver Wolf is the most generalist debuffer unit that can provide a Weakness Implant of the leftmost ally's element, while also providing further RES down if the enemy did not have that original weakness type before the implant. This provides a lot of utility for fights that require weakness breaking (like Apocalyptic Shadow), and Silver Wolf's damage amplification in a single-target scenario is potentially competitive with some of the strongest supports in the game if you are implanting a weakness type that is not naturally an enemy's own.

Build Notes

EHR: With an EHR body, SW only needs 3 substats to hit 66.7% EHR, making it easy to build SPD on her. You can alternatively use a HP/DEF body and build EHR instead, but it is only recommended for sustainless runs.

SPD: 160 SPD is a general SPD breakpoint for SW, you can aim for higher if needed (e.g. Cipher LC). If you are using Eagle, you can aim for 166.67 SPD (3 actions with 2 ults) or 183.2 SPD (3 actions with 1 ult).

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Lies Dance on the Breeze S1 BiS
Cipher's signature LC and SW's best LC. Provides 18% SPD and 24% DEF shred for 2 turns (8% more DEF shred and better uptime than Pearls); the SPD in particular is useful for hitting specific breakpoints like 173.4 SPD with Vonwacq, 183.2 SPD with Eagle or 200 SPD.
Resolution Shines as Pearls of Sweat S5 Alternative
Best F2P option for damage amplification, provides 16% DEF shred for 1 turn.
Before the Tutorial Mission Starts S5 Alternative
Great F2P option. While the EHR from this LC is mostly wasted and this LC provides no direct damage amplification, this LC enables a 2-turn Skill-Basic Ultimate rotation with an ERR planar, making SW's damage amp more applicable to enemies with summons.
Holiday Thermae Escapade S5 Alternative
Good BP option that provides 16% vulnerability, but due to SW's already high DEF shred in her base kit (45% on Ultimate + 12% from Talent), Pearls generally provides better damage amp than this LC unless enemies already have 100% DEF shred.

Relic Sets

Divine-Querying Master Smith BiS
Best supportive 4pc for strictly damage amplification. While SW does not provide much damage to the team (and hence the CD% increase is not useful), this set provides 15% DMG to all allies.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Minmax option for maximising the number of actions SW gets. SW can get 3 actions in 150 AV with 183.2 SPD and one ult (which is easy since E0 SW gets a 2-3 turn Ultimate rotation), and the SPD requirement can be lower with Vonwacq as well. Do note that if you do not get any additional actions through Eagle procs, this relic set provides no useful effects for SW.
2pc SPD / 2pc SPD Alternative
For if you don't have any of the above 4pcs.

Planar Ornaments

Lushaka, the Sunken Seas BiS
Good planar that provides SW with 5% ERR (needed to guarantee a 3-turn Ultimate) and a 12% ATK buff to one unit. Useful planar that is good for almost all teams in which you would use SW, but other ERR planars can be conditionally better.
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Good planar that provides ERR and allows Silver Wolf to take action to apply her debuffs before your DPS. Also useful for attaining 3 actions in 150 AV with a high speed SW or with Eagle.
Penacony, the Land of Dreams Alternative
Good planar that provides ERR and provides 10% DMG to all Quantum units in the team, which is specifically useful for Quantum DPSes like Archer, Seele or Rin.
City of Converging Stars Alternative
Decent planar option; does not provide ERR to SW so she needs to take a hit to get her Ultimate in 3 turns, but good damage amplification planar as it provides 12% CD to all allies after an enemy is defeated.

Investment Recommendation

Silver Wolf is already a complete unit at E0S0 (either with Pearls or Tutorial), and you are better off investing into other teammates instead of Silver Wolf if possible. However, S1 (Cipher's LC) and E1 are decent QoL and potential minmax upgrades for her, and her E2 is a small but decent team damage increase.
