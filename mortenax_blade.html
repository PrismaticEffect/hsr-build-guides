Mortenax Blade

"I remember you. Time has made us both sharper. Yet you are still you... and I am still me."
★★★★★

Character Summary

Mortenax Blade is a debuffer unit that relies on his Ultimate to activate an enhanced state to deploy his debuffs and enhanced attacks. He provides DMG%, DEF shred, vulnerability, and if he is paired with a second Nihility unit, he also provides further Ultimate DMG% (without a second Nihility unit he increases his personal DMG%). He also provides a debuff on every ally attack (which makes him premier support for Acheron teams), activates a follow-up attack after the team attacks enough times (or is hit often enough), and has defensive utility for the team due to his built-in damage reduction, high taunt value and naturally high HP.

Due to Mortenax Blade's notable damage amplification, personal damage and SP lightness (he is a SP neutral, leaning SP positive unit if played as such), he can be used in most teams that don't call for specific qualitative supports, but due to his ability to apply a debuff on every hit and his high attack frequency, he is best suited for either Acheron teams or teams that call for high attack frequency like Ashveil or Feixiao.

Build Notes

Recommended stats are out of combat and assumes 4pc Master Smith. Aim for lower HP if you are using Eagle/Vonwacq.

CR/CD: MBlade gains 20% CR in combat, so aim for 80% CR. Subtract 28% CD if you are using Bone Collection or Master Smith 4pc. Aim for lower CD if you're on Eagle. Alternatively, you can sacrifice CR/CD for more SPD if you want him to take more actions instead.

SPD: Aim for 140+ SPD so that Blade would take 2 actions before his enhanced state expires.

Rope: Use an ERR rope if you want to maximise the number of ults he gets, which is better for Acheron teams or if Blade is wearing Eagle. Otherwise, HP rope is comparable if not often better for personal damage.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Reforged by Hellfire S1 BiS
MBlade's signature and his most all-rounded LC. Provides 30% max HP, enough energy for Blade to deploy his Ultimate after his first turn (very big QoL), and 30% CD to all allies (and 60% CD for himself). In terms of team damage amplification, this LC is weaker than some premium options, but the general utility and increased personal damage this LC brings makes this one of his best options.
Lies Dance on the Breeze S1 Alternative
Cipher's signature LC and provides the strongest team damage amplification out of all LC options available assuming that you don't overcap on DEF reduction. Provides 18% SPD and 24% DEF shred, but MBlade has to reach 170 SPD to ensure the full 24% DEF shred, which compromises on MBlade's personal damage. More ideal when paired with an MBlade that's made for debuff frequency rather than personal damage, like using him with ERR rope or Eagle 4pc. In some scenarios, stronger than MBlade's signature, but it can also be 3-5% worse than his signature in other scenarios where MBlade's damage is more relevant. Building EHR is also suggested while using this LC but is not a must.
Before the Tutorial Mission Starts S5 Alternative
Great F2P option and a general comfort LC, though this LC does not provide any damage amplification by itself. This LC provides MBlade with 8 energy (affected by ERR) if he attacks an enemy with a DEF down debuff, which notably increases his Ultimate frequency -- which increases the overall attack frequency of the team, and it synergises well with a MBlade that is using Eagle 4pc as well. Note that outside of his enhanced state, he can only activate this LC's effect if there is another unit that provides DEF down like Ashveil. With ideal play this is only about 10% worse than MBlade S1, but it can do much worse in some scenarios.
Resolution Shines as Pearls of Sweat S5 Alternative
Great 4-star option and usually the best damage amplification non-premium LC. Provides 16% DEF shred on all allies with this debuff, and given MBlade's high attack and hit frequency, landing Pearls' debuff should be quite reliable even if you do not build much or any EHR on him. About 13-15% worse than MBlade S1 in simulations.

Relic Sets

Divine-Querying Master Smith BiS
MBlade's dedicated set and his general best set for both personal damage and team damage amplification. Provides MBlade with 12% HP, 28% CD and a teamwide 15% DMG increase (since he provides a DEF down debuff). Provides a 6% team damage increase over 2pc/2pc combos.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Compromises on MBlade's damage, but due to MBlade's high ult frequency (especially when paired with ERR rope and/or LCs like Tutorial), MBlade can wear this set to maximise the number of actions he takes, which in turn can lead to more attacks for batterying either Ashveil or Acheron. Can theoretically be better than Master Smith if MBlade's extra actions from this set gives an extra FUA or Ultimate from either MBlade, Ashveil or Acheron, but it is on a case-by-case scenario. With no additional actions, Eagle is strictly worse than any other set on the list.
Longevous Disciple Alternative
Good set that increases MBlade's personal damage (12% HP and 16% CR. Pioneer Diver of Dead Waters is of very similar strength to this set, but it requires 3 debuffs in total. About 2-3% worse than Master Smith.
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
If you don't have any of the above sets.

Planar Ornaments

Bone Collection's Serene Demesne BiS
MBlade's best planar for personal damage (12% HP and 28% CD).
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Generically good planar that provides 5% ERR for MBlade (useful for some breakpoints) and 12% ATK to a specific unit. This planar works better than Bone Collection if MBlade is more of a battery for Acheron or Ashveil rather than a damage dealer, but both Bone Collection and Lushaka provide very similar team performance.
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Good planar that is primarily used to make sure that Blade is the first unit to go first so that he could deploy his Ultimate as soon as possible (which unlocks the bulk of MBlade's damage amp and personal damage). The 5% ERR also helps with getting his Ultimate up as quickly as possible and for generating more Ultimates, but this planar provides no offensive utility to MBlade or the team otherwise.

Investment Recommendation

Mortenax Blade is already a very complete unit and, as of 4.3, one of the most versatile units in the game at E0S0, but his E1 and E2 are generally great eidolons for his dedicated teams. His S1 is also great QoL and frees up high demand LCs like Tutorial or Cipher LC as well. For Ashveil teams, I would personally go for E2 MBlade over any other investment first, but even E0S0/E0S1 is still fine. For Acheron teams, he is complete at E0S0/E0S1, and I would only go for E2 MBlade if you are willing to go for Ashveil as well. For teams outside of that, stopping at E0S0/E0S1/E1S0 is good enough.
