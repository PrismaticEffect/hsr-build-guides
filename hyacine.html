Character Summary

Build Notes

CD: For full Remembrance teams (Castorice or Evernight teams), you can aim for less CD and more SPD/HP, since Evernight provides a lot of CD for Hyacine as is.

SPD: 200 SPD is required to give Hyacine a 20% max HP increase. You need 18 SPD without her signature to get to 200 SPD, while you need 12 SPD with her signature. Subtract 6 SPD from recommended stats if you are using Warrior Goddess 4pc.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Long May Rainbows Adorn the Sky S1 BiS
Hyacine's signature LC and her best LC in terms of raw stats and overall utility. Provides 18% SPD for Hyacine, 18% vulnerability on memosprite skill, and burns all allies' HP on Basic/Skill/Ultimate use to trigger one instance of Hyacine's healing on all allies. The HP burn + healing also provides more personal damage for Hyacine and other potential mechanical changes for units like Castorice or Evernight.
Memory's Curtain Never Falls S5 Alternative
Most comfortable F2P option as it provides 12% SPD (which Hyacine really wants) and 16% DMG for the entire team on Skill use (which Hyacine should be using fairly often).
The Story's Next Page S5 Alternative
Great F2P option but requires more SPD on Hyacine to reach the 200 SPD requirement for Hyacine overall. Provides 32% max HP and 24% OHB, which can make this LC better than Memory's Curtain in terms of healing and Hyacine's personal damage with good enough relics.

Relic Sets

Warrior Goddess of Sky and Thunder BiS
Hyacine's dedicated set and her most comfortable option overall. Provides a total of 12% SPD (though it only shows 6% out of combat) and 15% CD to all allies, both stats that Hyacine wants while also providing good offensive utility for the entire team.
World-Remaking Deliverer Alternative
Minmaxing set for Hyacine. While this set does not provide any SPD, the 4pc increases Hyacine's max HP by 24% (great for healing) and provides 15% DMG to all allies, which is usually more valuable than Warrior Goddess' CD increase.

Planar Ornaments

Giant Tree of Rapt Brooding BiS
Hyacine's most comfortable planar to use as it provides SPD and OHB, which help with Hyacine's healing and personal damage. However, this planar does not provide any offensive buffs for the team.
Broken Keel Alternative
Decent planar for Hyacine, as it provides 10% CD to the team, and Hyacine gains 50% Effect RES through her major traces which easily fulfills Broken Keel's Effect RES requirement.
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Generically good planar for hypercarry DPS teams. Provides 12% ATK to one ally, and the ERR from this planar can also be useful for Hyacine as she can potentially save 1 SP per Ultimate rotation to get her Ultimate up with full uptime.

📊 Vertical Investment Analysis
Sig LC (S1)
★★★★
The vulnerability provides a 10-15% team damage increase for most teams, but the LC provides a lot of QoL for Hyacine as it makes her much easier to build and it frees up Memory's Curtain LC for Cyrene if you are using her. More importantly, this LC greatly improves her healing, which makes this a very significant (35% team damage) improvement for Castorice teams specifically. The HP burn effect is also useful for Blade and Jingliu teams as they rely on HP burn to get their rotations going.
Teammates
★★★★
For mono Remembrance teams, you are better off investing in other teammates past Hyacine E1S1. For ATK scaling teams, you are better off investing into your DPS or supports past E0S1 Hyacine.
E1
★★★★
20-30% team damage increase for purely HP scaling teams and a higher increase for Castorice teams, but otherwise an okay eidolon which improves Hyacine's personal damage for ATK scaling teams.
E2
★★★★
Provides 30% SPD for all allies, which is excellent for many teams like DoT or SW999 teams.

Investment Recommendation

Hyacine E0S0 is already a complete unit, but E0S1 provides a lot of QoL (in terms of building Hyacine and for sustain) and a small damage amp for all teams. E1 is a nice damage increase for HP scaling teams, and E2 is excellent for teams that benefit from SPD, but I would personally not go for E2 just for the SPD boost alone unless you really want to improve your SW999 team or you really like her.
