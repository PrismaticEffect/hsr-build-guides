The Dahlia

"My name is Constance, and those who have fallen to my charms call me 'The Dahlia.' Don't be afraid. Come with me. In this duet belonging only to the two of us, you will learn how the past is being cremated and how the future is being buried..."
★★★★★

Character Summary

The Dahlia is a Break-oriented support that provides an ally (called the "Dance Partner") with Weakness Break Efficiency and the ability to inflict Super Break DMG without reducing all of the toughness of an enemy. Dahlia also provides herself with a SPD boost, DEF shred, a decent amount of toughness damage and a weakness implant of her Dance Partner's element and her own on every use of her Ultimate. Additionally, The Dahlia provides a small BE% boost to her Dance Partner assuming that they have attained some healing or shielding, but this does not come up often since most break teams are currently being run sustainless.

For Dance Partners who are able to implant their weakness types (namely Boothill and Firefly), Dahlia also provides a substantial SPD boost to them, and for Firefly and herself (units who can implant Fire weakness) in particular, they gain energy based on the number of instances they have implanted a Fire weakness onto enemies, up to 50% of their max energy.

Due to Dahlia's ability to allow an ally to inflict Super Break DMG without breaking enemies, The Dahlia is considered an essential support for all Break DPSes since she provides a significantly larger window for these DPSes to deal damage rather than relying on an enemy who is Weakness Broken to deal damage. For Firefly in particular, Dahlia also reduces the amount of downtime Firefly has outside of her enhanced state to 1 turn, which allows Firefly to go back immediately into her enhanced state after one Skill use, which greatly enhances her overall damage output.

Build Notes

BE: Subtract 30% BE from the BE stat recommendation if you are using 4pc Kalpagni.

SPD: 156.2+ SPD with 1 DDD proc ensures 3 actions in cycle 0 of wave 2. Otherwise, there are no meaningful breakpoints for Dahlia, just aim for a proper balance between SPD and BE.

Rope: For personal damage, wear a BE rope + Cavalry. For better SP gen or more ultimates and general utility overall, use ERR rope + Eagle. Eagle is the easiest way for her to ensure 3 actions in cycle 0 (effective 200 SPD), but this might not be necessary for most players. Without Tutorial LC, ERR or BE rope is dependent on number of enemy targets; Dahlia can be played with a SBB rotation while still maintaining a 2t ult rotation with ERR rope + 2 enemies.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Never Forget Her Flame S1 BiS
Dahlia's signature LC and her general BiS LC. Provides 60% personal BE, 32% Break DMG (an extremely rare multiplier) to both herself and her Dance Partner, and 1 SP on every Ultimate use (which is great for SP heavy break DPSes like Firefly). About a 30% increase over Tutorial, though the difference is slightly smaller (20-24%) if you are using Pearls LC instead.
Lies Dance on the Breeze S1 Alternative
Cipher's signature LC and one of her stronger options; provides 18% SPD and 24% DEF shred if she exceeds 170 SPD, which is trivial due to Dahlia's self-SPD boost. Pairs well with other DEF shred sources like Fugue, Pearls LC (on Fugue), and Iron Cavalry 4pc. Can have comparable performance to Dahlia's LC if you are not overcapping on DEF shred, but generally about 25-26% better than Tutorial LC.
Resolution Shines as Pearls of Sweat S5 Alternative
Best damage amping 4* LC, provides 16% DEF shred to all allies. Dahlia does not need to build much EHR on this LC as this LC's debuff lands per hit rather than per attack, and Dahlia lands a lot of hits in particular. A 10-12% damage improvement over Tutorial LC on paper, but do note that if Fugue is wearing this, Dahlia cannot wear this LC to apply the debuff a second time.
Before the Tutorial Mission Starts S5 Alternative
Good F2P option for utility, Dahlia can get 2-turn Ultimates with a Skill-Basic rotation with this light cone even with a BE rope, which is useful for reducing toughness damage or activating Eagle as frequently as possible overall.
Solitary Healing S5 Alternative
Good F2P option for personal damage, provides Dahlia with 40% BE.

Relic Sets

Iron Cavalry Against the Scourge BiS
Dahlia's best relic set for personal super break damage and maximising her BE buff for her Dance Partner (assuming that you use a sustain), providing her with 16 BE% and DEF shred for her Super Break damage.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Best set for maximising the number of Dahlia actions in a fight, which might be useful for reducing toughness damage specifically. Dahlia can get her Ultimate every 2 turns with ERR rope or Tutorial LC, which gives her an effective 14.3% SPD increase. It is worth noting that Dahlia usually uses an ERR rope or Tutorial LC with this set. If you don't get any additional actions with Eagle or if you don't generate Dahlia's Ultimate frequently enough, this set is worse than a 2pc SPD/2pc SPD combo.
2pc SPD / 2pc SPD or BE Alternative
For if you don't have any good 4pcs of the above sets.

Planar Ornaments

Forge of the Kalpagni Lantern BiS
Unconditional BiS planar since you are always implanting a Fire weakness with Dahlia. Provides the highest BE% boost (40%) out of any planar while also providing 6% SPD.
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Good for frontloading actions and taking 3 actions in 150 AV of the first wave; The Dahlia needs 151.5 SPD or higher to take 3 actions within 150 AV.

Investment Recommendation

The Dahlia is already a complete unit at E0S0, but E1 and S1 are great improvements for any Break team, and E2 can be particularly strong for non-Firefly break DPSes. Firefly E0S0 + Dahlia E0S1/E1S0 + Fugue E1S0/E0S1 should be more than capable of clearing most endgame content at the moment, though you would need higher vertical investment on Dahlia and Fugue to have similar performance with E0S1 Rappa or Boothill instead.
