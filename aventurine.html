Aventurine

"Your humble servant "Aventurine," at your disposal. Just a cog in the machine known as the IPC's Strategic Investment Department. Nevertheless, I can also play the role of "friend" — if needed."
★★★★★

Character Summary

Aventurine is a shielder who deploys his shielding through his Skill and through triggering his followup attack, which requires allies with his shield to be attacked or partially through his Ultimate. His Ultimate also provides a small debuff which increases the amount of Crit DMG dealt to an enemy.

In a scenario where enemies do not deal much damage, Aventurine can be built as a speedy SP generator who provides shielding for the team while also providing good attack frequency (though his personal damage even when built for damage is small without notable vertical investment), which is useful for some teams like Ashveil teams or Feixiao teams. In scenarios where enemies hit less frequently but much harder, he can be built more defensively to keep the team alive while still providing okay attack frequency, though he might need to consume a SP every now and again.

Build Notes

DEF: This build is primarily for building him as a pure sustain, which is his current best build. Going over 4000 DEF is fine as it provides better shielding for the team.

CR/CD: At the moment, Aventurine does not provide enough personal damage to be worth building CR/CD on. However, you can still build CR/CD on him if you have him at higher investment (S1 and/or eidolons).

SPD: 160+ SPD is recommended to make him a good SP generator for most teams. However, you can aim for 133.34/142.86+ SPD as well.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Inherently Unjust Destiny S1 BiS
Aventurine's signature LC and his general best LC both in terms of shielding and offensive utility. Provides Aventurine with a high base DEF cone, 40% DEF, a bit of personal CD and 10% vulnerability to all enemies.
Moment of Victory S1 Alternative
Second best LC for Aventurine in terms of overall shielding, providing 48% DEF and a bit of aggro which could redirect a few more attacks to Aventurine (who is usually the tankiest unit in the team). However, this LC does not provide any offensive utility.
Day One of My New Life S5 Alternative
Best 4* defensive option, provides 24% DEF (though the base DEF of this LC is much lower than Aventurine's S1 or Moment of Victory) and a 12% damage reduction for all allies. However, this LC does not provide any offensive utility.

Relic Sets

Self-Enshrouding Recluse BiS
Aventurine's general BiS relic set as it provides shield strength and an additional team CD increase when allies are holding any of Aventurine's shields.
2pc Shield / 2pc SPD Alternative
If you don't have a good 4pc Recluse set. Can also use 2pc SPD/2pc SPD as well.
Pioneer Diver of Dead Waters Alternative
His BiS set for maximising his damage output assuming that he is wearing his S1, but his personal damage at the moment is not high enough to justify using a damage-oriented build on him at the moment.

Planar Ornaments

City of Converging Stars BiS
Good planar choice for Aventurine, best in teams have multiple allies dealing damage; while Aventurine does not benefit from the ATK boost from this planar, but the CD boost from this planar is stronger than that of Keel, though it requires an enemy kill for the CD boost to trigger.
Broken Keel Alternative
Very similar performance to Converging Stars and multiple allies can wear Keel unlike Converging Stars; this planar provides unconditional 10% CD to the entire team, and Aventurine can easily fulfill the Effect RES requirement of this planar as he gains 50% Effect RES in combat.
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Generic good planar choice for Aventurine when using him with a hypercarry DPS; provides a bit of ERR% and 12% ATK to a specific DPS.
