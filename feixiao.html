◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

I Venture Forth to Hunt S1 BiS
Feixiao's signature LC and generally her BiS LC. Provides 15% CR and 54% DEF shred to Feixiao's Ultimates specifically. Do note that when paired with MBlade (Tutorial LC) and Ashveil, you are already overcapping on DEF shred with this LC by 24%, so this LC ends up doing a bit worse in practice in her BiS team.
Worrisome, Blissful S1 Alternative
Topaz's signature LC and Feixiao's best supportive LC. Provides 18% CR, 30% DMG to all of her FUAs, and provides 24% CD to all allies when Feixiao uses a 2 FUAs on a specific enemy, which is easy to achieve.
See You At The End S5 Alternative
BP option that gives 40% CD and 40% DMG to all of Feixiao's damage. S2+ of this LC is better than Cruising assuming 50% uptime on the ATK boost.
Cruising in the Stellar Sea S5 Alternative
Best F2P option that provides Feixiao with 16% CR and 40% ATK for 2 turns if she defeats an enemy (though given her BiS team of 3 DPSes, this is not reliable). You can build around the secondary 16% CR buff of this LC, but it makes runs much less consistent and is not recommended.

Relic Sets

Eagle of Twilight Line BiS
A minmax option for Feixiao used to maximise her actions and attack frequency if optimised. Given that Feixiao's FUA trigger is reset on her turn, Feixiao wants to take as many natural turns as possible to maximise her attack frequency, which can be used to battery both Ashveil's Ultimate and MBlade's FUAs and Ultimate. At the moment, this set, if maximised, is considered much (10-15% team damage) stronger than Valorous 4pc, but if Feixiao gets no extra actions from this set, it would be worse than Valorous 4pc.
The Wind-Soaring Valorous Alternative
Feixiao's best 4pc for maximising damage per Ultimate specifically; provides 6% CR and 36% Ultimate DMG, in which her Ultimate is the majority of her damage.
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
For if you don't have any of the above 4pcs, 5-6% worse personal damage than 4pc Valorous.

Planar Ornaments

Duran, Dynasty of Running Wolves BiS
BiS planar for Feixiao's personal damage, provides 25% CD and 25% FUA DMG (the majority of Feixiao's damage).
City of Converging Stars Alternative
BiS planar for Feixiao in a dual to trio carry team, provides 24% ATK to Feixiao and 12% CD to all allies when an enemy is defeated. Do note that only one ally in the team can wear City of Converging Stars, so Feixiao has to wear a different planar if another ally (like Ashveil) is using it.
Izumo Gensei and Takama Divine Realm Alternative
Good planar that provides 12% CR and ATK to Feixiao in teams involving Ashveil. 3-4% worse than Duran in terms of personal damage.

Topaz's S1 provides about a 12-15% team damage increase over Cruising in a trio carry team.
Teammates
★★★★

E1
★★
A 25-30% personal damage increase, which converts to about a 10-12% damage increase in a trio carry team.
E2
★★★★
Greatly increases her charge rate for her Ultimate (especially in a team with Ashveil and Blade), which can lead to a very notable team damage increase due to more Feixiao personal damage and faster Ultimate cycling. Team damage increase can exceed 40%+ when paired with Eagle on Feixiao if optimised.

Investment Recommendation

At the moment, Feixiao needs both Ashveil and MBlade to stay relevant in the meta. However, when you have both Ashveil and MBlade, you are generally better off replacing Feixiao with a support like Tribbie instead, as Feixiao does not provide any buffs to the team beyond batterying Ashveil/MBlade slightly better than a supportive unit like Tribbie. If you wish to use Feixiao for longer while ensuring that Feixiao is a damage increase to the Ashveil/MBlade team, E2 Feixiao is highly recommended. S1 is generally of lower priority compared to her E2.
