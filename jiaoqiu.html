★★★★★

Character Summary

Jiaoqiu is a debuff-based support who provides general vulnerability and Ultimate DMG vulnerability through his Ashen Roast debuff (which also doubles as a minor Burn DoT). His Zone deployed by his Ultimate also inflicts his debuffs (at max stacks) onto any enemy that spawns, which makes him a fairly high QoL debuffer. His main specialisation is being able to apply Ashen Roast debuffs on enemy turns (up to 6 debuff applications per Ultimate use), which makes Jiaoqiu a great support for Acheron (at least on release) due to his ability to charge up Acheron's Ultimates much faster than other supports at the time.

Beyond his specific synergy with Acheron, his vulnerability and overall damage amplification falls short out of both existing premium debuffer units and buffer units, despite being theoretically usable in any team.

Build Notes

EHR: Jiaoqiu needs 140% EHR to ensure that his Ultimate adds Ashen Roast debuff stacks onto 30% Effect RES enemies when they take action and to max out his A4. Higher EHR is better (177.8% max) but not necessary.

SPD: You can aim for 166.7 SPD for 5 actions in 300 AV or 3 actions in 150 AV with Eagle, but the relic investment is fairly steep.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Those Many Springs S1 BiS
Jiaoqiu's signature LC and his best LC overall. Provides a massive 60% EHR and an additional 24% vulnerability, which notably increases his damage amp.
Lies Dance on the Breeze S1 Alternative
Cipher's signature LC and theoretical BiS LC as it provides 18% SPD and 24% DEF shred, but since this LC does not provide EHR, building up to 140% EHR is quite difficult as it requires 18 EHR substats in total.
Eyes of the Prey S5 Alternative
Best 4* option as it provides 40% EHR and a slight DMG increase to Jiaoqiu's DoT damage, but this is primarily used as an EHR stat stick. Tutorial Mission S5 will be better than this LC with a DEF shred source, but you are better off using Tutorial Mission on other units like Silver Wolf.
Solitary Healing S5 Alternative
Great option for Pure Fiction-like scenarios since Jiaoqiu can gain a lot of energy through this LC, which lets him use more Ultimates, which leads to more debuff application overall. However, this LC does not provide EHR, which can reduce the chance of JQ applying his Ashen Roast debuffs on enemy turns against higher Effect RES enemies.
Resolution Shines as Pearls of Sweat S5 Alternative
Budget option of Cipher's LC. Good damage amplification overall as it provides 16% DEF shred, but does not provide EHR for Jiaoqiu.

Relic Sets

Eagle of Twilight Line BiS
Theoretical best effective SPD increase for Jiaoqiu (9.1% SPD increase), and the SPD increase can be higher with additional energy sources like Solitary Healing S5 or energy refills from various enemies.
2pc SPD / 2pc SPD Alternative
For if you don't have any good Eagle 4pc for Jiaoqiu.

Planar Ornaments

Sprightly Vonwacq BiS
Best planar for maximising actions, as it provides 5% ERR (which is useful for some energy breakpoints if you're using a full basic rotation with Jiaoqiu), and it allows Jiaoqiu to take 3 actions in 150 AV for the first wave assuming a 156.7 SPD Jiaoqiu with Eagle (or 173.4 SPD without Eagle). Also useful as it allows Jiaoqiu to apply his Zone first before any other units.
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Best supportive planar for Jiaoqiu as it provides 5% ERR and provides 12% ATK to the first unit of the team.
