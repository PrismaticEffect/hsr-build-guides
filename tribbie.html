Character Summary

Tribbie is a AoE HP-scaling support unit that provides RES PEN, vulnerability, and Additional DMG procs to the enemy with the highest HP based on the number of targets hit in an attack. Tribbie can also deploy an AoE follow-up attack every time an ally (other than herself) uses their Ultimate, though she can only deploy one FUA for each ally until her Ultimate is used.

Tribbie gains most of her energy through allies attacking enemies, with her gaining more energy as more enemies get hit. As a result, Tribbie is most often used in AoE-heavy scenarios, as she can deploy her Ultimate very frequently (which is good for deploying her FUAs often and also for LCs and effects that require Ultimate use like Dance! Dance! Dance!). However, Tribbie can be used as a generalist support or a support that attacks frequently to battery other units like Feixiao, Ashveil or Mortenax Blade.

Build Notes

HP: If you are giving Tribbie Bone Collection, she will reach 5000+ HP with a HP orb and HP boots to gain an additional 28% CD.

CR/CD: Subtract 32% CR from the CR recommendation if you are using Poet 4pc and 28% if you are using Bone Collection 2pc. If you are building a faster Tribbie (to make her generate more energy for DDD procs), you can focus less on building CR/CD instead.

SPD: If you are buidling Tribbie more as a way to activate DDD more, you can build more SPD on her instead at the expense of CR/CD.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

If Time Were A Flower S1 BiS
Tribbie's signature LC and her best LC for personal damage and damage amplification. Gives herself 36% CD out of combat and the team (including herself) 48% CD in combat, gets her Ultimate immediately at the start of combat, and increases her energy gain per FUA to maximise her attack frequency.
Dance! Dance! Dance! S5 Alternative
Potentially great to BiS option for Tribbie; Due to Tribbie's high Ultimate frequency, Tribbie is one of the best users of DDD, which can lead to many teamwide advances for the team, leading to additional turns for certain team members. If you do get extra turns on your DPSes, this LC is potentially Tribbie's best in slot LC. Otherwise, this provides no offensive utility (and damage increase) to the team and you're better off using other LC options.
Meshing Cogs S5 Alternative
Good F2P option for attack frequency; Provides no direct offensive utility to Tribbie, but she gains 8 additional Energy every time she lands an attack (including FUAs), which potentially increases her attack frequency if the rest of the team uses their Ultimates frequently.

Relic Sets

Poet of Mourning Collapse BiS
Tribbie's BiS set for personal damage as it provides 10% DMG and 32% CR to Tribbie (Tribbie can easily meet the sub-95 SPD requirement as you generally do not build SPD on her). In terms of personal damage, about 8-10% better than other alternatives.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Best set for maximising actions in particular to maximise Ultimate frequency (and in turn DDD procs). If you do not care much for Tribbie's personal damage, Tribbie can be used solely for her ability to use DDD efficiently, and this set is used to ensure that she can take upwards of 3 actions in 150 AV with Eagle + DDD.
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
For if you don't have any of the above 4pcs. You can use 2pc Crit / 2pc HP for personal damage, or 2pc Crit / 2pc SPD for maximising Ultimate frequency/DDD procs.

Planar Ornaments

Lushaka, the Sunken Seas BiS
Generically good planar that provides 5% ERR for Tribbie (useful for some breakpoints) and 12% ATK to a specific unit. This planar works better than Bone Collection if Tribbie is more of a battery rather than a more damage-oriented support unit.
Bone Collection's Serene Demesne Alternative
Tribbie's best planar for personal damage (12% HP and 28% CD).
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Good planar that provides 5% ERR and useful for deploying Tribbie's Zone early (if Tribbie needs to take her turn to get her Ultimate up) or for maximising the number of actions Tribbie takes in Wave 1, though the latter is more useful if Tribbie is also wearing Eagle 4pc due to her low SPD.
City of Converging Stars Alternative
Generically good supportive planar that provides 12% CD to all allies when an enemy is defeated. Other supportive planars like Broken Keel or Fleet of the Ageless are also good generic supportive planars for Tribbie.

Investment Recommendation

Tribbie is a decent unit at E0S0 but is lacking damage amplification compared to Mortenax Blade (the current best general damage amper) and does not provide any qualitative improvements for any existing teams other than The Herta (which MBlade does a similar but better job of other than being unable to use DDD). Tribbie becomes a strong generalist unit at E1S0 due to the additional 24% True DMG buff, but the damage amp can fall short compared to supports that provide qualitative improvements to the team like Cyrene for Anaxa or Yaoguang for Elation teams. If you like using Tribbie, E1S0 is the generally recommended stopping point, but E1S1 is also good for some niche scenarios.
