Phainon

★★★★★

Character Summary

Phainon is a hypercarry DPS who removes all of his allies but himself from the battlefield when he enters his enhanced state, which requires Coreflames (his special energy system) to charge. He obtains Coreflames through enemies hitting him or allies buffing him. In Phainon's enhanced state, he has massive self-buffs (making him near unkillable in his enhanced state), implants Physical weakness onto all enemies, gains an enhanced skill (meteor attack) that does notable bounce + distributed damage, a counter that advances all enemies and deals damage based on the number of enemy actions that he has caused, and an enhanced basic attack.

Given his survivability in his enhanced state, his best teams are primarily sustainless teams. Phainon's gameplay loop generally involves Phainon accumulating 12 Coreflames as quickly as possible through his supports (to minimise the amount of damage the supports take and to clear quickly) and then using Phainon's 3 different attacks based on the enemy encounter (number of enemies, enemy mechanics, etc.). This makes Phainon a fairly linear character to play on the surface, but optimising his gameplay (buff retention, enhanced skill management, etc.) can be difficult depending on the supports that you use with him or the enemies that you face.

Build Notes

CR: Phainon's supports provide him with various amounts of CR, so build your Phainon around your supports so that he reaches 100% CR in combat. If you are using Sunday, aim for 80% CR. If you are using Cyrene/RMC, aim for 74% CR.

SPD: This SPD recommendation assumes that you are running either Cerydra/Sunday/Sparkle or Cyrene/Cerydra/RMC. There are other team setups that might prefer a 133.34 SPD setup, but Phainon's best two teams prefer Phainon to be on ATK boots.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Thus Burns the Dawn S1 BiS
Phainon's signature LC and his best LC by some distance. Provides Phainon with very high base ATK (excellent since Phainon has a lot of ATK self-buffs), 12 base SPD (useful for some fights), 60% DMG and 18% DEF shred. Provides a 20% damage increase generally over his F2P option, but the damage improvement is higher (28-35%) in very short fights (like in wave 1 cycle 0) as the base SPD can help Phainon squeeze in an extra turn in 150 AV for example.
On the Fall of an Aeon S5 Alternative
Best F2P option which provides 64% ATK and 24% DMG if Phainon breaks an enemy, which is quite doable since he implants Physical weakness onto all enemies in his enhanced state.
Something Irreplaceable S1+ Alternative
Very comparable to Aeon if Aeon is being used in another team; has a higher base ATK than Aeon but only provides 24% ATK and 24% DMG at S1. This LC outperforms Aeon at higher superimpositions.

Relic Sets

Wavestrider Captain BiS
Phainon's dedicated set and his best relic set by about 5% over Scholar. Provides Phainon with 48% ATK and 16% CD since the 48% ATK buff from Wavestrider is permanent on Phainon when he enters his enhanced state.
Scholar Lost in Erudition Alternative
Weaker but more efficient alternative 4pc. Provides 8% CR, 20% unconditional Skill/Ultimate DMG and an additional 25% Skill DMG on Phainon's first Enhanced Skill (meteor).
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
For if you don't have any of the above 4pcs.

Planar Ornaments

Arcadia of Woven Dreams BiS
The planar with the highest ceiling for Phainon as it provides 36% DMG (Phainon is considered the only friendly unit on the battlefield in his enhanced state). About a 3-4% improvement over Rutilant with Sunday/Sparkle, but about a 6% improvement over Rutilant with Cyrene/RMC.
Rutilant Arena Alternative
Decent planar which provides 8% CR and 20% DMG to his Enhanced Basic and Skill.
Bone Collection's Serene Demesne Alternative
Provides 28% CD to Phainon in his enhanced state since his max HP is well above 5000. Slightly worse than Rutilant Arena, but can be used if you are farming this planar for other DPSes.

Investment Recommendation

E2 Phainon is the team's most important vertical investment and should be prioritised over any other vertical investment once you have the full team (either Sunday or Cyrene variant) at E0S0. Beyond that, E1/S1 Cerydra, S1 Cyrene and E2 Cyrene can be considered to make your Phainon team stronger. Sunday's eidolons are quite weak and only his S1 is worth investing into at most, so invest into Cyrene over Sunday if you plan on making your Phainon team stronger in the long-term.
