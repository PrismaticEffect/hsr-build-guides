★★★★★

Character Summary

Mydei is a HP-scaling blast DPS who relies on his enhanced state, where he increases his max HP, enhances his attacks to do more damage while draining his own HP. His main source of damage revolves around enabling his enhanced Skill Godslayer Be God, which is usually only triggered after Mydei accumulates enough Vendetta charge (through losing HP, either from enemy attacks or HP drain sources like his base kit, his LC or other allies).

In addition to being a very tanky DPS due to his high max HP, he can also taunt enemies with his Ultimate and revive himself multiple times in combat, making him a fairly comfortable DPS to run sustainless as long as his supports have high effective HP as well.

Build Notes

This build assumes that you are running Cyrene + Hyacine (HP boots), which is currently his best core team.

HP: Aim for 8000 HP to maximise his HP -> CR conversion, but don't go too much over 8000 HP if possible to maximise GBG frequency. DMG orb is preferred if Mydei has his S1.

CR/CD: Mydei gains 48% CR in combat if he is at 8000 HP. Subtract 28% CD if Mydei is using Bone Collection.

SPD: Most of Mydei's attacks in the Cyrene/Hyacine team is out of turn, so you do not need to build SPD on him. If you are using Sunday instead of Cyrene, build 133.34+ SPD on him instead, but you might need to wear a HP body at E0S0 if you are building SPD on him.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Flame of Blood, Blaze My Path S1 BiS
Mydei's signature LC and his overall best LC due to its mechanical changes for Mydei. Provides 18% HP, 60% DMG, and helps Mydei drain his HP faster due to the HP burning effect of this LC, which enables him to utilise more Godslayers through Vendetta charges. 20-24% better than the F2P option (Ninja Record: Sound Hunt), though the difference is larger in Sunday teams.
A Secret Vow S5 Alternative
A good cheap alternative LC for Mydei as it provides at least 40% DMG and an additional 40% DMG if the enemy's HP% is lower than that of Mydei's, which is quite achievable for at least 50% of the fight due to his natural HP consumption. However, this requires Mydei to build more HP% through substats to reach 8000 HP. About 5-10% better than Ninja Record, though the difference is larger in sustainless runs specifically.
Ninja Record: Sound Hunt S5 Alternative
F2P option and one of the few F2P Destruction LCs that provide Mydei with the stats that he wants (CD and HP).

Relic Sets

As Navigator Isee Sees It BiS
Mydei's best set but only in Cyrene teams (which is his current BiS team) which provides up to 54% Skill and Ultimate DMG, which is all of his damage. Mydei can stack this set's buffs up to 3 times (for 54% DMG) fairly quickly with Cyrene since most of his Skill uses would be out of turn, though there is still a bit of rampup time on this compared to Scholar. Regardless, in 350-450 AV fights, this is a 3-5% improvement over Scholar 4pc, but the difference is slightly smaller in shorter fights. This set does not work in Mydei/Sunday teams since Mydei takes many turns in that team, which means that you will never be able to fully stack this set's 4pc effect.
Scholar Lost in Erudition Alternative
A good and efficient set to farm for Mydei. Provides 8% CR and 20% unconditional Skill and Ultimate DMG (and slightly higher Skill DMG after using an Ultimate).
Longevous Disciple Alternative
Decent set which provides 12% HP and 16% CR, but you risk overcapping on CR due to Mydei's CR self-boost. About 3-4% worse than Scholar if you are not overcapping on CR.
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
If you don't have any of the above 4pc options. 2pc Crit / 2pc HP works as well.

Planar Ornaments

Bone Collection's Serene Demesne BiS
BiS option which provides all of the stats that he wants (12% HP and 28% CD).
Rutilant Arena Alternative
Great option which provides 8% CR and 20% Skill DMG (the majority of Mydei's damage), but you risk overcapping on CR with this planar if you are pairing this with Scholar 4pc or Longevous 4pc. However, assuming that you are not overcapping on CR, this planar is comparable to Bone Collection.

Mydei E1 > Tribbie E1 (replace RMC) > Mydei S1 > Hyacine E1 > Cyrene S1. Recommended stopping point is Mydei E1S1 + Tribbie E1S0 + Cyrene E0S0.
