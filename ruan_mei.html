Ruan Mei

★★★★★

Character Summary

Ruan Mei is a support that provides Weakness Break Efficiency (toughness damage), a tiny bit of SPD, DMG%, and in her Ultimate, RES PEN.

While Ruan Mei's direct damage amplification is weaker than more contemporary supports such as Mortenax Blade, Ruan Mei is still used as a budget support for break-oriented teams or fights that require a lot of toughness damage due to her being one of the two supports (at E0) to provide WBE. She can also be used in sustainless break teams due to her ability to delay enemies for longer than any Break support, though at the expense of lower damage amplification compared to other break-specific supports.

Build Notes

BE: 180% BE is a requirement and not a recommendation if you are using a crit-based DPS. If you are using a break DPS, you can aim for lower BE especially if you have a LC that doesn't increase BE.

SPD: You can build Ruan Mei at a high SPD for her to generate more SP for the team, but it provides lower ult uptime even with Meshing Cogs S5 or MOTP S5 (3t ult rotation). Otherwise, you can build Ruan Mei with 120 or 133.34 SPD with Vonwacq to maximise ult uptime but with less SP generation.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Past Self in Mirror S1 BiS
Her signature LC and a good generalist option for all teams. Provides 60% BE to herself (making her easier to build), DMG% on Ultimate use, energy at the start of each wave, and provides an SP every Ultimate use, which can be useful for some teams. Do note that the DMG% boost does not affect break teams, so other LCs might provide better utility or damage amplification for those teams.
In Pursuit of the Wind S5 Alternative
Harmony BP option and best offensive LC for break teams. Provides Break DMG% to all allies, which is a DMG modifier that is both rare and one of the few DMG modifiers that affect Break damage.
Memories of the Past S5 Alternative
Provides 56% BE to Ruan Mei and personal energy gain every time she uses a basic, which ensures a SP positive 3-turn Ultimate rotation.
Meshing Cogs S5 Alternative
A cheaper option to Memories of the Past S5, ensures a SP positive 3-turn Ultimate rotation but does not provide any BE%.
Dance! Dance! Dance! S5 Alternative
Conditionally good LC on Ruan Mei. If you get extra turns on your DPS with this LC, it is potentially a good option for Ruan Mei. Otherwise, this provides no offensive utility and greatly reduces Ruan Mei's Ultimate uptime, so you're better off using a different LC instead.

Relic Sets

Watchmaker, Master of Dream Machinations BiS
Best offensive support set for Ruan Mei, provides 16% BE for Ruan Mei and 30% BE to all allies for 2 turns whenever Ruan Mei uses her Ultimate. However, uptime on this buff is fairly limited since Ruan Mei can only get her Ultimate up every 3 turns at best.
2pc SPD / 2pc BE Alternative
For if you don't have a good 4pc Watchmaker set or if you're not using Ruan Mei in a break team. You can also use two 2pc BE sets or two 2pc SPD sets as well.

Planar Ornaments

Sprightly Vonwacq BiS
Best planar for maximising actions and activating Ruan Mei's Ultimate before any allies take a turn. Activating RM's Ultimate first before any allies take action help with maximising overall team damage since all of the teams' attacks will get RM's RES PEN buff.
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Generalist planar that provides a bit of ERR% for Ruan Mei (which is useful for reaching some energy breakpoints) and a bit of damage amplification for Firefly or non-break DPSes.
City of Converging Stars Alternative
Provides the highest damage amplification for crit-based DPSes but requires an enemy kill. For non-crit DPSes (like break or DoT), this planar provides no offensive bonuses.
