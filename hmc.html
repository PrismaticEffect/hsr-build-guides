Character Summary

Harmony MC (or HMC for short) is a Break support that provides Super Break damage to all allies for 3 turns when their Ultimate buff is deployed. HMC also provides Break Effect to all allies and deals decent toughness damage on their own with their Skill.

HMC is the only non-premium unit that provides Super Break damage to allies, which makes HMC an excellent budget support for most Break teams (though The Dahlia is still an essential support for all break teams). In sustainless teams, HMC also provides enough Super Break DMG to be generally the third best support for break teams (assuming low investment on Ruan Mei). HMC also gains energy through allies (including themselves), which makes HMC an overall excellent support for deploying buffs or utility through Ultimate use such as Watchmaker 4pc (which is a supportive set for all Break units) or Dance! Dance! Dance! LC.

Build Notes

Recommended stats assume 4pc Watchmaker and a BE rope.

BE: Subtract 40% if you are using Kalpagni 2pc.

Rope: You get a 3-turn SBB ultimate rotation with a BE rope and E6 HMC. However, you can use an ERR rope for 2-turn Ultimates if you use a Skill spam rotation + get 3 breaks every 2 turns, but this is more doable only in Pure Fiction-like scenarios.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Past Self in Mirror S1 BiS
Ruan Mei's signature LC that provides a good combination of utility and BE% amp. Provides energy to all allies at the start of each wave, 60% BE (which converts to 9% BE to all allies), and given that HMC can use their Ultimate every 2-3 turns, this LC is also good for providing SP as well.
Dance! Dance! Dance! S5 Alternative
Given that HMC can get their Ultimate every 2-3 turns, HMC can trigger this LC quite often, which can lead to many teamwide advances in a fight, which can lead to additional turns for the team (or reduced ult downtime with Firefly if speed tuned properly). If you do get extra turns on your break DPS or reduce ult downtime with certain break DPSes, this LC is potentially HMC's best in slot LC. Otherwise, this provides no offensive utility (and damage increase) to the team and you're better off using other LC options.
In Pursuit of the Wind S5 Alternative
Harmony BP option and best offensive LC if you don't get any additional turns through DDD. Provides Break DMG% to all allies, which is a DMG modifier that is both rare and is one of the few DMG modifiers that affect Break damage.
Memories of the Past S5 Alternative
Provides 56% BE to HMC and personal energy gain every time HMC uses a Basic or Skill, which ensures a SP positive 3-turn Ultimate rotation for HMC.

Relic Sets

Watchmaker, Master of Dream Machinations BiS
Best offensive support set for HMC, provides 16% BE for HMC and 30% BE to all allies for 2 turns whenever HMC uses their Ultimate. Given that HMC gets their ultimate every 2-3 turns, the teamwide BE buff should have reasonably high uptime for most teams.
2pc SPD / 2pc BE Alternative
For if you don't have a good 4pc Watchmaker set. You can also use two 2pc BE sets or two 2pc SPD sets as well.
Eagle of Twilight Line Alternative
Primarily a minmax option to maximise HMC's effective SPD if you are using DDD on HMC and are able to get consistent 2-turn Ultimates with HMC. With 3-turn Ultimates, you get a 9.1% SPD increase, but you get a 14.3% SPD increase with 2-turn Ultimates.

Planar Ornaments

Forge of the Kalpagni Lantern BiS
HMC's general best planar for offensive support as it provides HMC with 6% SPD and 40% BE as long as HMC attacks an enemy with Fire weakness (which should usually be happening since you are running HMC with Dahlia).
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Best planar for maximising actions and activating HMC's Ultimate before any allies take a turn. HMC can easily take 3 actions in the first wave with DDD + Vonwacq (requires 156.4 SPD with 1 DDD proc), and activating HMC's Ultimate first before any allies take action also help with maximising overall team damage since all of the teams' attacks will get HMC's Super Break damage buff.
Talia, Kingdom of Banditry Alternative
A weaker variant of Forge that provides 36% BE maximum, but you do not need to attack enemies with Fire weakness to use this planar.
