Character Summary

Build Notes

CR/CD: Dr. Ratio gains 15% CR and 30% CD in combat. Reduce the stat recommendations by 8% CR/24% CD if you are using Pioneer 4pc. You can aim for lower CD if you are using Genius 4pc instead.

SPD: You can use base SPD if you are pairing Dr. Ratio with Sparkle, but 133.34+ SPD is otherwise recommended.

Genius is better than Pioneer Diver with more DEF shred sources, such as S1, Ashveil or MBlade.

◈ Recommended Light Cones / Relic Sets

Light Cones

Baptism of Pure Thought S1 BiS
Dr. Ratio's signature and his best LC. Provides 44% CD (assuming 3 debuffs, which Ashveil and/or Mortenax Blade will provide), 36% DMG and his FUAs (the majority of his damage) ignore 24% DEF. In terms of personal damage, this is a 24-25% personal damage improvement over Cruising with Ashveil, which is closer to a 12-15% team damage increase overall (higher improvement if Dr. Ratio does more personal damage than Ashveil).
Worrisome, Blissful S1 Alternative
Topaz's signature and good supportive LC. Provides Dr. Ratio with 18% CR and 30% FUA DMG, and provides 24% CD to all allies. 5-6% team damage increase over Cruising.
See You at the End S5 Alternative
BP option, providing 40% CD and 40% FUA/Skill DMG (which is the bulk of Dr. Ratio's damage). 3-4% team damage increase over Cruising.
Cruising in the Stellar Sea S5 Alternative
Best F2P option. Provides 16% CR and 40% ATK for 2 turns when Dr. Ratio gets a kill, which isn't very consistent when paired with other damage dealers like Ashveil or MBlade.

Relic Sets

Pioneer Diver of Dead Waters BiS
Best unconditional relic set, providing 8% CR, 24% CD and 12% DMG assuming that there are 3 debuffs present.
Genius of Brilliant Stars Alternative
Conditionally BiS relic set for Dr. Ratio; provides 20% DEF shred against Quantum-weak enemies, but only 10% DEF shred against non-Quantum weak enemies. With both Ashveil and MBlade, this set is his BiS relic set (by about 2% team damage).
2pc Crit / 2pc Crit Alternative
For if you don't have any of the above 2pcs. Any 2pc that gives DMG for Dr. Ratio works as well, such as Wastelander, Pioneer or Duke.

Planar Ornaments

Duran, Dynasty of Running Wolves BiS
Dr. Ratio's strongest planar for personal damage (provides 25% DMG and CD) overall. BiS planar if Dr. Ratio is doing the majority of the damage in the team (like in hypercarry or in less invested teams with Ashveil).
City of Converging Stars Alternative
One of the strongest options for dual/triple carry teams as it provides 12% CD to all allies while also providing 24% ATK for Dr. Ratio (which he lacks in the team). Do note that the CD boost can only be applied once, so only one unit in the team can wear City of Converging Stars (since Ashveil also likes this planar). Similar performance to Duran, though City will be better with more investment on Ashveil and/or MBlade.
Izumo Gensei and Takama Divine Realm Alternative
Good planar that provides 12% CR and ATK to Dr. Ratio only if there is another Hunt character present (like Ashveil). 2% worse than City/Duran.
Inert Salsotto Alternative
Provides 8% CR and 15% Ultimaet and FUA DMG, 3% worse than City/Duran. Firmament Frontline: Glamoth (at 135 SPD) is of similar strength to this.

Ratio's best team; Ashveil provides personal damage, DEF shred and a notable CD increase to all FUAs, while Mortenax Blade provides personal damage, further DEF shred/vulnerability and AoE damage coverage for the team. Dr. Ratio helps the team by providing personal damage and high attack frequency which batteries Ashveil's Ultimate and MBlade's Talent, which requires 9 attacks to trigger his FUA. Investment Recommendation

Recommended stopping point for Dr. Ratio is E0S1, but in general, if you plan on using Dr. Ratio for longer periods, you are better off investing into FUA supports instead, such as Ashveil (E1S1) or MBlade (E2S0/E2S1).
