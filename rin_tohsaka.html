Character Summary

Rin is an SP-heavy bounce (sub-)DPS that utilises a special energy called Gem Energy to inflict massive amounts of bounce damage. She accumulates Gem Energy through her Ultimate, her allies (including herself) generating or consuming SP, and she also generates more Gem Energy through using her Enhanced Skill, which consumes all SP until there is 2 left and converts the consumed SP into Gem Energy. As a result, as a DPS, she wants to be paired with a support that can provide lots of SP (namely Sparkle), and ideally a unit that could duplicate her Enhanced Skill uses like Cerydra.

Rin also has a special Joint FUA with specifically Archer, where Rin generates extra SP for Archer whenever Archer uses his Skill at a specific amount of SP (or after his 5th Skill use in a turn). She also provides very strong buffs (a massive amount of ATK and RES PEN) to Archer in particular, which makes Rin a very potent support/sub-DPS for Archer specifically.

Build Notes

CR/CD: Build your Rin based on how fast you want your Rin to be. If you want a slower Rin (112.94+ SPD), you can build more CR/CD on her (~140% CD). If you are building her with SPD, focus more on building CR and SPD over CD.

SPD: You can either build Rin at 112.94+ SPD out of combat (133.34+ in combat) or faster than Sparkle in Archer teams (her speed should be Sparkle's speed - 20.4). If you are running a hypercarry Rin team, run her at base SPD.

Light Cones

Flickering Stars S1 BiS
Rin's signature LC and her best overall LC for both hypercarry DPS and a sub-DPS for Archer. Provides 18% CR and 72% Skill DMG to herself, and provides 20% DEF shred to all units when any unit consumes 4 or more SP (which is trivial with either Rin or Archer). A 12% damage increase for Archer teams over the F2P option (Eternal Calculus), and a 30% damage increase for Rin hypercarry teams.
Life Should Be Cast Into Flames S1 Alternative
Anaxa's signature LC and Rin's second best LC for a sub-DPS Archer. This LC provides 12% DEF shred and 10 additional Energy for Rin, which makes it easier for her to get her Ultimate (to inflict vulnerability and generate 1 SP), but it does not provide any personal damage bonuses for Rin. This LC also allows Rin to wear Divine-Querying Master Smith 4pc, which provides an additional 15% DMG to the team, which if combined with this LC, makes this LC very comparable to her signature LC for a sub-DPS Rin specifically.
Today is Another Peaceful Day S5 Alternative
BP LC that provides 64% DMG to Rin, which she particularly wants out of any other stat. THerta's S1 is of very comparable performance to this LC (the DMG buff from THerta's LC is limited), though it makes Rin easier to build as a hypercarry due to the CR that THerta's S1 provides.
Eternal Calculus S5 Alternative
Best F2P option for a sub-DPS Rin in 3+ target scenarios as it provides SPD for Rin, which makes it easier for her to outspeed Sparkle in some scenarios, which is good for Archer's SP expenditure. For personal damage, her best 4* option is Geniuses' Repose.

Relic Sets

Genius of Brilliant Stars BiS
Provides the highest damage amplification when paired with her S1 and/or enemies are Quantum weak. 8% better than 2pc/2pc combos in terms of personal damage, but less than 2% difference otherwise.
Scholar Lost in Erudition Alternative
Good and efficient relic set to farm. Provides 8% CR to Rin (makes her easier to build since she needs a lot of CR) and 20% DMG to her Skill and Ultimate, which is the bulk of her damage. Comparable to Genius without Quantum weakness, but 5% weaker than Genius with Quantum weakness, with a larger difference with more DEF shred sources.
2pc Crit / 2pc SPD Alternative
For if you don't have 4pcs, or if you want a Rin that is faster than Sparkle specifically.

Planar Ornaments

City of Converging Stars BiS
Great sub-DPS planar that provides 24% ATK to Rin and 12% CD to all allies when an enemy is defeated. 3% team damage improvement over a planar with no supportive effect.
Penacony, The Land of Dreams Alternative
Great sub-DPS planar that provides a small amount of ERR% (doesn't reach any notable ult breakpoints but it helps) and DMG% to both Rin and Archer, which is a valuable stat for them. 3% team damage improvement over a planar with no supportive effect.
Lushaka, the Sunken Seas Alternative
Good sub-DPS planar that provides a small amount of ERR% (doesn't reach any notable ult breakpoints but it helps), but due to how much ATK Archer has when paired with Rin, this planar's ATK boost is only about a 2% team damage improvement over a planar with no effect.
Sprightly Vonwacq Alternative
Good planar for frontloading actions, particularly important if your Sparkle is also on Vonwacq and you plan on having a Rin that is faster than Sparkle. However, this planar provides no damage amplification for Rin or the team.
Tengoku@Livestream Alternative
Best DPS planar that provides 48% CD to Rin. Rutilant Arena is also a good but weaker option that makes it easier to build CR on Rin.

Investment Recommendation

For Archer hypercarry teams, E0S0/E0S1 Rin is already a very complete unit for him and will be sufficient to clear most forms of endgame content when paired with Sparkle. As a hypercarry team, E2S1 Rin is most recommended, but she would want E0S1 minimum to clear content (though slower than an Archer/Rin team would with similarly invested teammates).
